# Privacy Policy — ROVEN
Last updated: 2026-11-04

Phase 1 of this site collects no personal data and has no accounts, analytics or ads.
The only thing stored is your language choice, saved in your own browser (localStorage) and never sent anywhere.
Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of loading them.

When the store launches (orders, accounts, payments), this policy will be updated before any data is collected, to cover what is collected, why, how long it is kept, and how to ask for deletion.
