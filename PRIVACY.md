# Privacy Policy — ROVEN
Last updated: 2026-11-06

This page explains what the ROVEN website collects and why. Before launch, fill in the two items marked TODO and have the text reviewed by someone qualified in your country.

## What we collect
- **When you place an order:** your name, phone number, city, address, and the items you ordered. We use them only to prepare, deliver and confirm your order and to contact you about it.
- **If you pay by transfer:** the receipt image you upload, so we can confirm your payment.
- **If you leave a review:** the rating, the text, and the name you choose to show. Reviews appear on the site only after we approve them.
- **Visit statistics:** we count visits on our own database: which page, the page language, the device type (mobile, tablet or computer), your country (taken from the request, not stored together with your IP address), and which website you came from. A random code identifies your visit only while the browser tab is open. We store no name and no IP address with these counts, and we do not count you if your browser sends "Do Not Track".
- **Your language choice and your cart,** saved in your own browser and never sent to us until you place an order.

## Who handles the data
- Orders, receipts, reviews and visit counts are stored in Supabase.
- Product photos are hosted on Cloudinary. Customer data is not stored there.
- When an order arrives, a message with the order details (name, phone, address, items) is sent to the ROVEN team on Telegram.
- The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep technical server logs (such as IP addresses) to run the service. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when loading them.

## Payments
We do not collect card details on this site. Payment is cash on delivery or by transfer to the accounts shown at checkout.
